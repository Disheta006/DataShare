Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about DataShare

You can transfer mobile data in just a few simple steps:

  1. 1. Log in to your account.
  2. 2. Enter the mobile number of the person you want to send data to.
  3. 3. Choose the amount of data you want to transfer.
  4. 4. Confirm the transaction.

Once confirmed, the data will be transferred instantly and the receiver will receive a notification.

Currently, our platform supports Jio network users.

We are actively working on expanding support to other major telecom providers in the future to make data sharing accessible to more users.

Small data transfers (for example 5 MB ‐ 150 MB) can be shared free of cost between users.

For larger transfers (above the free limit), a small service fee may apply to maintain the platform and support network integration.

All charges, if applicable, will always be shown clearly before confirming the transfer.

Security is one of our top priorities.

Our platform ensures:

  1. 1.Encrypted transactions
  2. 2. Secure account authentication
  3. 3. Verified mobile number transfers

This ensures that your data is transferred only to the intended recipient safely and reliably.

If a transfer fails:

  • • The data will not be deducted from your account, or
  • • It will be automatically refunded if it was already processed.

You can also contact our support team or WhatsApp support if the issue continues. Our team will quickly review and resolve the problem.

Once a transfer is confirmed and processed, it cannot be cancelled because the data is immediately delivered to the recipient’s network.

However, if the transaction fails or does not complete successfully, no data will be deducted from your account.
